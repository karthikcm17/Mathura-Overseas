A Decade of Honest Guidance

Why Mathura Overseas

For over 10 years, we've helped Indian students find a genuine, NMC-compliant path into medicine abroad — without donation seats, without guesswork, and without disappearing once the admission letter arrives.

Our story

Built on one simple idea — do right by the student

Mathura Overseas started because too many capable students were losing a real shot at a medical career simply because an Indian government seat wasn't within reach that year — and too many of the "abroad MBBS" options being sold to them were vague on fees, vague on recognition, and nowhere to be found once the first installment was paid.

Over the past decade-plus, we've built our practice the other way around: we only work with universities we can vouch for, we quote the full fee structure upfront, and our team — based right here in Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu — stays reachable throughout the years a student is actually abroad, not just through the admission process.

That approach has taken more than 200 students from a NEET scorecard to a medical degree abroad, and a good number of them are now practicing doctors — some back in India after completing their licensing requirements, others continuing their careers overseas.

Our mission

Every student deserves an honest shot at medicine

A NEET score that falls short of a government seat isn't the end of a medical career — it's a fork in the road. Our mission is to make sure that fork leads somewhere real: a recognised degree, a fair fee, and a support system that's still there in year four, not just at admission. If a family is considering MBBS abroad, we want them making that decision with complete information, not a sales pitch.

What we look for in every university
  • NMC FMGL Regulations 2021 compliance
  • WHO / ECFMG / recognised accreditation
  • English-medium instruction throughout
  • A track record with real, verifiable Indian students on campus
  • A published, stable fee structure — no annual surprises
